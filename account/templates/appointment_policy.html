Appointment Booking, Rescheduling, Cancellation, and Walk-In Appointments Policy

This policy outlines how appointments are booked, rescheduled, and canceled in our Electronic Health Records (EHR) system, and how walk-in patients are handled. It also includes essential system requirements and communication guidelines.

Appointment Booking Policy

Booking Method: Patients can book appointments by selecting an available date and time using our online EHR portal.

Slot Availability: All appointment slots are first-come, first-served.

  • A date/time slot is considered taken and unavailable for other bookings if it already has an appointment scheduled—regardless of its status (e.g., pending, confirmed, completed).
  • Slots only become available again if the appointment is canceled by the patient (before confirmation) or by the clinic.
  • A patient is not allowed to book for the same service on the same day. If they wish to book for the same service, they should select a different date.

⚠️ Tip: Always check the calendar for open slots before planning your visit.

Appointment Rescheduling Policy

Direct rescheduling is not allowed. To change an appointment:

  • The patient must cancel their current appointment (only if it's not confirmed).
  • Then, book a new appointment with the updated date and time.
  • If the appointment is already confirmed, the patient must contact the clinic to request rescheduling.

Appointment Cancellation Policy

1. Cancellations by Patients

  • Patients may cancel pending or unconfirmed appointments via the EHR portal.
  • Confirmed appointments cannot be canceled directly by patients in the system.
  • To cancel a confirmed appointment, patients must contact the clinic.
  • If the patient fails to do so and does not attend the appointment, it will be marked as a no-show.

2. Cancellations by the Clinic

The clinic may cancel any appointment due to:

  • Emergencies
  • Provider unavailability
  • Administrative adjustments

If this occurs:

  • The patient will be notified via email.
  • The email will include instructions to rebook a new appointment at their convenience.

Notification and Communication Policy

The EHR system will automatically notify patients via email of the following appointment status changes:

  • Confirmed
  • Completed
  • Canceled
  • Missed (No-show)

To receive these updates, patients must:

  • Provide a valid and accessible email address during registration or appointment booking.

Patients who wish to stay informed without logging into the EHR system are strongly encouraged to keep their email addresses up-to-date.

📢 The clinic is not responsible for missed updates due to invalid or inaccessible email accounts.

Walk-In Appointment Policy

To maintain accurate appointment records and ensure smooth patient flow, the following policy outlines how walk-in patients are handled within our Electronic Health Records (EHR) system.

1. Walk-In Without an Existing Appointment

Patient Account Requirement:
All walk-in patients must have a registered clinic patient account in order to book an appointment through the EHR system.

New Patients (No Account Yet):

  • Patients who do not yet have a clinic account will be asked to register on-site during their visit.
  • After registration, they must log in to the clinic's EHR system using their new credentials.

Booking the Appointment:

  • Once logged in, the patient will be asked to book an appointment using the system interface.
  • The system will record this appointment in the database, marking it as an official scheduled appointment for the day.

⚠️ Walk-ins are still subject to availability. The clinic cannot guarantee immediate service if slots are fully booked.

2. Walk-In With an Existing Appointment

Early Service Request:
If a walk-in patient already has a scheduled appointment but wishes to be seen earlier on the same day due to availability:

  • The patient must log in to their account via the clinic's system.
  • They will then be required to cancel their originally scheduled appointment.
  • After cancellation, they must book a new appointment that reflects the actual time and date of their walk-in.

This process ensures:

  • Accurate and up-to-date appointment records.
  • Proper slot management for both clinic staff and other patients.

❗The original appointment must be cancelled in the system before the walk-in service is officially recognized and recorded.

📌 Important Notes

  • Clinic staff may assist with account creation or logging in, but patients are responsible for ensuring their appointment is properly entered into the system.
  • Failure to book the walk-in appointment through the system may result in no official record, which can affect medical documentation and follow-ups.
  • Appointments, even for walk-ins, are subject to availability of time slots and attending providers.

Other System Requirements and Concerns

Patients must ensure they:

  • Maintain accurate contact information (especially email).
  • Monitor their inbox (and spam/junk folder) for system messages.
  • Contact the clinic for any assistance regarding booking, cancellation, or technical difficulties with the EHR portal.
